Privacy

What we keep,
and what we don't.

No accounts. No cookies. No advertising, no tracking, nothing sold or shared.

Visits. We count visits with Plausible, which works without cookies and stores no personal data. We see numbers, not people.

Writing to us. A message sent through the contact form reaches our inbox with the email address you give. We use it only to answer you.

Submitting work. Open call submissions go through Tally. We receive your name, email address, portfolio link and the work you send, and use them only to review your submission and to contact you about it.

Running the site. Our host keeps technical logs, including IP addresses, for a short time, to keep the site safe and working.

Your data. Want to know what we have, or want it removed? Write to us and it will be done.
