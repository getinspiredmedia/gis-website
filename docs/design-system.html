Tiles

Work, never chrome

Images sit at reduced brightness and grayscale until you approach them. The name appears on a gradient, not on a card. Tiles never carry buttons, the whole tile is the target.

Ask for as little as possible

Writing rules

The filter every line passes
  • Short declarative sentences. Contrast carries the meaning, adjectives do not.
  • Concepts speak for themselves. If a line explains what a thing is, cut it.
  • State facts, not benefits. Twenty places beats a promise about exposure.
  • Every claim must be true today. Nothing that is in motion gets written as if it exists.
  • Never a long dash. Use a comma, a full stop, or a line break.
  • Never the word community in public copy.
  • No lists built on no this, no that, no the other.
  • No marketing register, no urgency, no pressure on a call to action.
  • No feature explanations in the shape of this lets you do X.

Accessibility floor

Not optional
  • White on blue and white on ink only. Ink on blue fails contrast and is never used.
  • Visible focus ring on every interactive element, in the current text colour.
  • All motion sits inside a reduced motion query. On view stops rotating and keeps its arrows.
  • Alt text in English, with the maker's name where the image is a work.
  • Fields carry real labels. Placeholders are examples, never the only label.
